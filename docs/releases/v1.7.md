## New in this version

### Pictures
- **Paste, drop or open a picture** to read it: a screenshot, a photo, a picture copied in a browser.
  - **Paste** (⌘V) works wherever the cursor is, from Scan or Create. With the cursor in a text field, copied text is still pasted as text.
  - **Drop** a picture anywhere on the window: a file, a picture dragged out of a browser, or a screenshot's thumbnail.
  - **Open** one with the folder button next to **Photo** (⌘O), or with **Open With** in Finder.
- **Read as a photo is.** The picture's biggest code goes in the field, its other codes are outlined to click, and its text can be selected right on it.
- **Back to Camera**, or Esc, returns to the camera. With a code in the field, Esc first shows the picture's text.
- **Opened on a picture, ShenzhenQR leaves the camera off** until you go back to it.
- **Zoom.** On a photo or a picture, pinch or scroll with ⌘ held to zoom in where the pointer is, up to 10×, then scroll to move around.

### Create
- **Wi-Fi codes.** For a QR code, **Wi-Fi network** makes a code that phones scan to join a network. Type its name and password, or click **Use Current Wi-Fi** to take this Mac's: macOS asks once for Location, as it counts Wi-Fi names as location, then for your Mac's password before sharing the Wi-Fi password.

### Scanning
- **The window fits the camera's picture**, so there are no black bars beside or above it.

### Command line
- `shenzhenqr wifi --ssid <name> --password <p>` makes a Wi-Fi code; `--current` takes this Mac's network, `--print` gives the code's text only.
